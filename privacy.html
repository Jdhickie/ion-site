Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 May 2026

This privacy policy explains how Íon ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information you provide through íon.ie.

Íon is operated by John Daniel Hickie, a sole trader based in Ireland. For any privacy-related questions, contact: ionmediaeire@gmail.com

What we collect

When you submit the contact form on íon.ie, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The service you're interested in (if selected)
  • The message you write
  • The time you submitted the form

We do not use tracking cookies or analytics on íon.ie.

Why we collect it

We use this information solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Provide quotes or proposals if you ask for them
  • Maintain a record of past enquiries

We do not use your data for marketing without explicit opt-in. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising.

Where it's stored

Your submission is stored in two places:

  • Supabase — our database provider, hosted in the EU
  • Resend — our email delivery service, which sends the reply on our behalf

Both providers are GDPR-compliant. The data does not leave their systems for any other purpose.

How long we keep it

We retain enquiry data for 12 months from your last contact with us. After that, it is deleted.

If you'd like your data deleted sooner, email ionmediaeire@gmail.com and we'll remove it within 30 days.

Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct any inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission of Ireland (dataprotection.ie)

To exercise any of these rights, email ionmediaeire@gmail.com.

AI-assisted responses

Replies to your enquiry may be drafted with the assistance of Claude, an AI model developed by Anthropic. Your message is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a draft reply, which is reviewed before sending. Anthropic does not retain this data for training purposes.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Material changes will be communicated by email if you've previously submitted an enquiry.
